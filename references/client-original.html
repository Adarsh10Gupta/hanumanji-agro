Our story

A legacy built on land, trust and trade.

Hanumanji Agro is built on a family agricultural-trading legacy that began in 1976 in Kalimpong. Our grandfather started by purchasing fresh ginger and other agricultural produce directly from farmers and trading it through local markets. From the beginning, the business depended on knowing the produce, understanding the seasons and building relationships based on trust.

Our father carried that legacy forward and expanded beyond ginger into large cardamom, Majista (Manjistha), traditional brooms and other regional commodities. The business grew into wholesale trading, supplying agricultural mandis and buyers across India. Each generation has added experience while staying connected to the growers and the agricultural character of Northeast India.

Today, Hanumanji Agro works to build on that foundation with a more structured approach to sourcing. Our interests include fresh ginger, turmeric and seasonal produce, premium spices, dried and dehydrated products, and selected botanical raw materials. We aim to connect farmers and regional suppliers with traders, processors, institutional buyers and wider markets.

Our ambition also includes supporting value chains for corporate processors—for example, sourcing ginger for businesses that make ginger paste—and engaging with institutional and government-linked agricultural initiatives. Our family’s experience includes work associated with initiatives involving the Government of Jharkhand and SIMFED, the Sikkim-linked marketing federation, including ginger seed/rhizome supply efforts intended to support farmers.

Hanumanji Agro’s broader agricultural trading work sits alongside a separate brand focused on herbal plants, botanicals and dehydrated ingredients such as Majista, Timur, orange peel, lemon peel, dried beetroot and dried bitter gourd. Together, these activities reflect our belief that the region’s agricultural knowledge and natural resources can serve a wider range of markets when matched with reliable sourcing and clear communication.

Carrying a family legacy forward

The next generation

A family legacy. A forward-looking vision.

From corporate experience to agricultural entrepreneurship.

Shivam Singh, proprietor of Hanumanji Agro, represents the next generation of a family business that began in Kalimpong in 1976. After studying Cost and Management Accountancy (CMA), he worked for three years at EY, gaining professional experience before making the decision to leave that career and return to the family enterprise.

His decision was driven by a long-standing ambition to build something meaningful in agriculture: to combine the family’s practical, generational knowledge of produce and trade with a more structured, professional approach to sourcing, communication and business relationships.

Shivam’s goal is to help connect farmers and regional suppliers with corporate processors, wholesale buyers and institutional customers. This includes opportunities to supply agricultural raw materials to processors—for example, ginger for ginger-paste manufacturing—as well as participation in government and institutional initiatives that can help farmers access inputs and markets.

The family’s experience includes work associated with the Government of Jharkhand and SIMFED, a Sikkim-linked marketing federation, including ginger seed/rhizome supply initiatives. These experiences reinforce the importance of building dependable connections between agricultural communities and larger supply chains.

Alongside Hanumanji Agro’s agricultural commodities, spices and seasonal produce, a separate brand focuses on herbal plants, botanicals and dehydrated ingredients including Majista (Manjistha), Timur, citrus peels, dried beetroot and dried bitter gourd.

“Our roots are local. Our experience is generational. Our ambition is to connect Northeast India’s agricultural wealth with dependable buyers and wider domestic and international markets.”

Our vision, mission & values

Trade built on trust. Growth rooted in nature.

Our purpose is to help the agricultural richness of Northeast India reach the people and businesses who value it—while respecting the growers, knowledge and relationships behind every product.

🌱

Our Vision

To become a trusted and recognised name in agricultural commodities, spices, dehydrated products and botanical raw materials. We want to help showcase the agricultural potential of Northeast India and connect the region’s growers and products with dependable buyers across India and, over time, international markets. We see a future where regional produce can reach more customers through stronger supply links, product understanding and long-term partnerships.

🧭

Our Mission

To connect farmers, growers, processors, traders, institutional buyers and businesses through clear communication and practical sourcing solutions. We aim to understand the needs of each customer, identify suitable produce, discuss quality and quantities transparently, and build relationships that can grow over time. We also want to explore opportunities to supply raw materials to processors and support initiatives that create meaningful market connections for farmers.

🤝

Our Values & Commitment

We value trust, consistency, responsible sourcing, regional knowledge and respect for the people who grow and handle agricultural produce. We aim to discuss quality, grading, availability, packaging, pricing and delivery terms clearly, and to confirm specifications for every order. We believe in realistic commitments, reliable communication and relationships that benefit growers, suppliers and buyers alike.
